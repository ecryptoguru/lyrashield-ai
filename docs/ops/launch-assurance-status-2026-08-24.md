## Launch decision

The deployed open beta is healthy, but code and runtime evidence are not interchangeable with external proof. General paid launch remains blocked while any hard-stop gate above is `INCONCLUSIVE` or while founder-controlled live billing and Deep acceptance decisions remain outstanding.
