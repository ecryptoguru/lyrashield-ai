# AI assurance framework mapping

Mapping version: `ai-assurance-mapping/1.0.0`
Recorded: 2026-08-14
Scope: OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025 only

This is a deterministic **readiness mapping, not certification or legal advice**. It stores stable OWASP identifiers and LyraShield-authored rationales only. A `DETECTED` deterministic signal is `OBSERVED`; accepted, current operational evidence is `EVIDENCE_ACCEPTED`; missing, clean, inconclusive, stale, or unsupported evidence is `NOT_ASSESSED`; and out-of-scope controls may be `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

The mapping uses OWASP LLM01 through LLM10 identifiers from the [OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025](https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-top-10-for-llm-applications-2025/). It does not claim the OWASP controls are completely assessed, passed, or compliant.

NIST AI RMF, MITRE ATLAS, and EU AI Act readiness mappings remain disabled until a recorded security/compliance-owner approval covers their exact source version, scope, owner, and review date.
