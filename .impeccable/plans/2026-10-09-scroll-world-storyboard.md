## Shared visual preamble

A continuous architectural evidence workspace for LyraShield AI: precise machined
navy surfaces, translucent structured evidence panels, restrained cyan routing,
soft directional studio light, clear depth and generous negative space for real
HTML interface overlays. Palette: #08111c, #0e1a28, #203246, #edf6fb, #00bae6,
#f3b95f. Premium product cinematography, controlled reflections, coherent scale
and materials, no people, no lettering, no logos, no invented interface text.
Amber represents incomplete evidence, never successful verification. Each space
opens naturally into the next along the same forward axis.

## Shot list

1. **Launch gate.** Wide opening onto the connected workspace. Slow forward push
   through a sculptural threshold, with the foreground separating to reveal the
   retained-record plane. Keep the headline area quiet. Existing account CTA is
   available immediately; the film never gates it.
2. **Target.** Three source objects converge inside a bounded transparent plane.
   Glide alongside the boundary before returning to the forward axis. Overlay
   real target-scope content; no literal readable UI baked into the video.
3. **Review.** Parallel paths enter the record plane. A small crane-up reveals
   their bounded extent, then settles forward. Real dashboard preview explains
   what was checked; visual completion is never a claim of complete coverage.
4. **Evidence.** Move closer to one evidence object that stays identifiable by
   shape and material. Overlay EX-014 and the real finding-detail presentation.
   A restrained partial orbit returns to forward drift before the handoff.
5. **Approval.** Approach a separated proposed-change layer. It remains visibly
   distinct from the retained record. The camera passes beside the boundary;
   nothing automatically applies, merges or turns green.
6. **Retest.** Two provenance planes align for comparison. A deliberate gap stays
   visible where coverage is incomplete. Advance through the comparison toward
   the report workspace; use real HTML for the inconclusive state.
7. **Report.** Evidence layers settle into a composed record, with the limitations
   plane retained. Slow arrival and final dwell; Insufficient evidence stays
   legible. Give the account action and plan choices a clear exit from the film.

## Mandatory motion handoff

Each leg prompt begins: “Continue the same slow, steady forward glide.”
Each ends: “In the final second, settle back into a slow, steady forward glide
toward the next opening.” The first leg establishes the initial view. Each
later leg receives the previous rendered leg's actual final frame; it must not
restart from its independent concept still. Do not pass a wide end-image that
forces the camera to pull back.

Leg duration assumption: 8 seconds, subject to the chosen endpoint's live schema.
Estimated total film duration: 56 seconds. Scroll distances are separately tuned;
visitors control playback, can reverse, and can skip directly to product/plans.

Review each handoff frame before rendering its successor. Inspect both sides of
every seam and fast/reverse scrubbing before accepting the final chain. Poster
fallbacks use actual accepted frames. Generated imagery remains illustrative;
current dashboard captures and semantic HTML carry product truth.
